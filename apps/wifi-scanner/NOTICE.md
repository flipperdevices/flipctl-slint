# Third-party material in this bundle

The table that names client devices by how they build their probe and association
requests, `src/taxonomy.rs`, is compiled into the program. It is generated by
`tools/taxonomy.py` from the signature database of wifi_taxonomy
(https://github.com/NetworkDeviceTaxonomy/wifi_taxonomy, commit ef24579), file
`taxonomy/wifi.py`:

    Copyright 2015 Google Inc.

used under the Apache License 2.0. It was changed: each signature is cut into its
probe and association halves, kept only where a half names one device and carries HT
capabilities, and stored as a hash with the device's name and a kind of our own. The
license text is in `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`.
